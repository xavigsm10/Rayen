# 🚀 Rayen v0.6.7 — Nuevos Ajustes de Calidad de Sonido y Problemas de Reproducción Solucionados

En esta versión **v0.6.7** nos enfocamos en dos cosas: que elijas cómo suena tu música y que ninguna canción se niegue a sonar.

---

### 🎚️ Nuevos Ajustes de Calidad de Sonido
* **7 niveles de calidad a elegir**: Dolby Atmos, Hi-Res Audio Max (hasta 24-bit / 192 kHz), Hi-Res Audio (24-bit / 96 kHz), CD Lossless (16-bit / 44.1 kHz), Standard 320 kbps, Data Saver 96 kbps y YouTube Music.

* **Controles siempre visibles**: Los botones del reproductor y la barra de volumen ya no quedan debajo de los botones de navegación del sistema.

## 🇺🇸 English

# 🚀 Rayen v0.6.7 — New Sound Quality Settings & Playback Issues Fixed

In this **v0.6.7** release we focused on two things: letting you choose how your music sounds, and making sure no song refuses to play.

---

### 🎚️ New Sound Quality Settings
* **7 quality levels to choose from**: Dolby Atmos, Hi-Res Audio Max (up to 24-bit / 192 kHz), Hi-Res Audio (24-bit / 96 kHz), CD Lossless (16-bit / 44.1 kHz), Standard 320 kbps, Data Saver 96 kbps and YouTube Music.
* **Independent download quality**: New separate setting for downloads — stream in one quality and save your songs in another.
* **Your choice is honored**: Player and downloads use the quality you picked, with automatic migration from the previous setting.

### 🎵 Playback Issues Fixed
* **Goodbye "Playback error (403)"**: New playback engine with updated cipher, per-client headers and automatic server switching when one is refused. Songs that failed to open now play on the first try.
* **Faster, steadier loading**: Bounded-range fetching at line rate plus a 6-minute playback buffer — fewer stalls, shorter waits, better survival on weak connections.
* **Controls always visible**: Player buttons and the volume bar no longer hide under the system navigation buttons.

---

### 📦 Installation Instructions
1. Download the attached APK (`Rayen-v0.6.7.apk`).
2. Open the file on your Android device and install the update (all downloaded songs, playlists, and settings will be preserved).
3. Open Rayen and enjoy your music!
